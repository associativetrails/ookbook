## About this file

This file lists every Kindle edition of Terry Pratchett's Discworld (and related) books found on Amazon.co.uk when searching for "Terry Pratchett Discworld" in the Kindle Store (Kindle eBooks category). Prices are the **buy price only** — Kindle Unlimited free access is excluded. Pre-orders are noted. Foreign-language editions are listed in a separate section.

## Notes and caveats

- Prices were captured on **24 June 2026** and will fluctuate. Amazon adjusts Kindle prices regularly, sometimes daily.
- The £0.99 price on *Unseen Academicals* is almost certainly a temporary promotional price.
- Several titles show "Best Price in 30 Days" banners (*The Light Fantastic*, *The Wee Free Men*), suggesting they have recently dropped.
- Items marked as "Included with Kindle Unlimited" but also showing an "Or £X to buy" price have been recorded at the buy price.
- Items that were **KU only** (showing £0.00 with no buy price) have been **excluded**.
- Foreign-language editions (Spanish *Mundodisco*, German *Scheibenwelt*) are excluded from the main tables above. They ranged from approximately £4.49 to £9.49.
- Two pre-orders appeared: *The Discworld Bestiary* (Paul Kidby, Oct 2026, £14.99) and *Thief of Time: The Discworld Graphic Novel* (Sept 2027, £10.99).
